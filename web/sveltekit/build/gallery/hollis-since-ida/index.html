Terms on this page

NAVD88:
an elevation above a fixed survey reference (the North American Vertical Datum of 1988), not a water depth.
MLLW:
mean lower low water, the average of each day's lower low tide; NOAA tide readings are measured against it.
flood event (FloodNet):
in FloodNet's record, a series of depth measurements greater than 10 mm at the sensor. The depth is measured at one point and does not show the depth on every part of the street. Riprap counts the events FloodNet's data marks as verified by a person.
Measured, Modeled, Proxy:
how directly a source observes flooding. Measured sources record it; modeled sources simulate a scenario; proxy sources, such as 311 complaints, indicate it indirectly.

This is an automated hazard-exposure briefing produced by Riprap from live and precomputed data sources. It is informational only and not a substitute for a professional risk assessment. These are public records about a place, not an assessment of any property or of the people who live there. Riprap computes no score or rating, and records can be missing where people report less.
