In brief

The surface of the ground within 150 m of this address ran 12.4°F warmer than the city's land average over 18 clear summer Landsat images (surface temperature, not air temperature).1 4.5% of the ground within 500 m is under tree canopy and 75.8% is paved or built over on the city's 2017 land cover map.2 Its neighbourhood, Hunts Point, scores 5 out of 5 on the Health Department's Heat Vulnerability Index, a rank among neighbourhoods and not a measurement.3

Terms on this page

Measured, Modeled, Proxy:
how directly a source observes heat. Measured sources record it; modeled sources estimate or forecast it; proxy sources indicate it indirectly.

This is an automated hazard-exposure briefing produced by Riprap from live and precomputed data sources. It is informational only and not a substitute for a professional risk assessment.

During a heat emergency the city lists its cooling centers at finder.nyc.gov/coolingcenters; heat safety guidance is at nyc.gov/health.
